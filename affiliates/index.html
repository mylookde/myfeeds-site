Questions partners ask

Can I try MyFeeds before I recommend it?

Yes, and you will. The program is for people who use MyFeeds, so your free Pro license comes first. You run the full plugin on your own site before you tell anyone about it. The free version is on WordPress.org too.

Do I have to post anything?

No. There is no quota and no deadline. Recommend MyFeeds when it fits what you already teach.

When and how do I get paid?

Monthly by PayPal, once your balance reaches $100. You see your balance in your partner dashboard at Freemius, the platform that runs the MyFeeds checkout, and we send the payout from there. Until you reach $100, your balance carries over to the next month.

How long do I earn from one customer?

As long as their subscription stays active. Every monthly or yearly payment counts. If they cancel, it stops.

Do I need a website?

Yes, a WordPress site to run your free Pro license on. You don't have to recommend MyFeeds there: social channels, newsletters, podcasts and email all count, and your coupon code works where links can't.

What does my audience pay?

Starter is $9 a month or $89 a year, Pro $19 a month or $199 a year, E-commerce $49 a month or $499 a year, each for one site. All plans side by side.

Can I recommend the free version too?

Yes. The free plugin lives on WordPress.org. If a reader installs the free version and later upgrades to a paid plan, the upgrade still counts for you within the 60 days after their click.

Is 30% the only rate?

30% on every payment is the standard. If you have a large audience in WordPress, blogging or affiliate marketing, ask us about individual terms.

Can I recommend other plugins too?

Yes, there is no exclusivity. Recommend whatever genuinely helps your audience.
