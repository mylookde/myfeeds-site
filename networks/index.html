What each network gives you.

Five work with a link, four with a file. MyFeeds, our WordPress plugin, reads both. Rakuten needs the Pro plan. Sovrn has no product feed.

NetworkYou getIn MyFeedsGuide
A link
AWIN A link you create. One for each shop. Paste the link. AWIN guide
Partnerize A link. One for each feed. Paste the link. Partnerize guide
Webgains A link or a file. Paste the link. Fallback: upload the file. Webgains guide
Admitad A link you generate. Admitad doesn't say how long it lasts. Paste the link. Fallback: upload the file. Admitad guide
Tradedoubler An address you build from a token and the shop's feed number. The standard one stops at 1,000 products. Paste it. Untested with a live account. Tradedoubler guide

CJ A file. To your inbox or by FTP. Upload the file. CJ guide
Impact A file you download. Impact calls it a product catalog. Upload the file. Impact guide

Rakuten No link. Its product files sit behind a login. Pro Connect directly. Fetched again every night. Rakuten guide

Check your first card. Not every route is tested with a live account. Each guide says which.

Link or file: what's the difference?

Stays current

A link

  • MyFeeds reads it again every night.
  • Cards in your published posts follow the shop's prices and stock.
A snapshot

A file

  • Prices and stock stay as they were on upload day.
  • Upload a newer file and your published cards follow.

Offered both? Take the link.

Amazon works differently.

Amazon has no feed file. Both ways need the Pro plan and your own Amazon Associates account.

Before Amazon opens its API

By link or ASIN

Paste a product link or its ASIN and name the product. It shows as a text card or a dot on a Shop the look photo.

  • No Amazon picture and no price.
Once it does

Live from Amazon

Products come live from Amazon's own Creators API. Amazon opens that access to associates with ten qualified sales in thirty days (as of October 2026, Amazon's documentation).
