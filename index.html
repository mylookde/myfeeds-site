What pasting links by hand costs you

You want the products part of a post to look as good as everything you wrote around it.

  1. Readers see links, not products

    A row of links asks readers to go and look somewhere else before they know what you picked.

  2. Every product takes minutes by hand

    Copy the link, save the image, retype the price, repeat. A roundup eats the time you wanted for the next post.

  3. Prices and stock go out of date

    Prices move and products sell out. Your best post keeps sending readers to a page that no longer exists.

The same post, with MyFeeds

Your readers see products, not links.

The products sit right where you write about them. Readers tap the one they like and land in the shop through your affiliate link.

  • Prices refresh every night.
  • When a shop runs a sale, your card shows it, old price struck through.
  • With Pro, MyFeeds tells you when a product you linked is no longer sold, and which post it's in.

Questions before you start

Still deciding? These are the ones people ask first.

Does this work with my affiliate network?

If your network gives you a shop's product feed link, MyFeeds reads it. AWIN, CJ, TradeDoubler, Impact, Rakuten, Webgains, Admitad, Pepperjam, Partnerize, FlexOffers and Sovrn are recognised out of the box, and other CSV, TSV, XML and JSON feeds work too. Amazon has no feed file, so on Pro and up its products come live from Amazon's own Creators API; Amazon opens that access to associates with ten qualified sales in thirty days. How to get a feed link from your network.

MyFeeds keeps growing, so that every affiliate can bring in the product feeds of the shops they work with. Is your platform missing? We can connect more platforms on request: write to business@myfeeds.site.

How is this different from a link plugin?

A link tool helps you paste links, one product at a time. A product feed hands you a shop's whole catalogue, prices and stock included: you search it inside the editor, pick in seconds, and the cards are updated every night, everywhere. Your readers see product cards they can buy from instead of a list of links.

Do I have to pay to get started?

No. The free version lives on WordPress.org and covers one feed, the smart search, the grid layout, and manual plus nightly sync. Upgrade only when you outgrow it.

What happens after the trial?

The Starter and Pro trials are 7 days, the E-commerce trial is 14 days. The checkout asks for a card or PayPal to start it, and you can cancel from your dashboard before it ends. If you don't continue, your plugin falls back to the free tier. Your existing feeds, posts and configuration stay intact.

What happens when a product sells out or is removed?

Every plan refreshes prices from the shop's feed every night. On Pro, MyFeeds also tells you when a product you linked is no longer sold and which posts still show it, so you can swap in another one.

What if a shop's feed looks unusual?

It still works. MyFeeds recognises the format on its own, and when a shop leaves a column out or empty, it looks for the same information elsewhere in the row. A messy feed doesn't break your import.

How long does setup take?

Most setups land in five to ten minutes. Paste the product feed link of a shop you're an affiliate for, and MyFeeds imports it. Open the block editor, type two letters, drop a card in.

Will it slow down my blog?

Products are stored in your own WordPress database and the cards are built from there, so your posts don't wait on a product service to answer.

Do I need technical skills?

No code, no command line. If you can install a WordPress plugin and paste a link, you're set.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes, any time, right from your WordPress dashboard. Your feeds and posts stay as they are.
