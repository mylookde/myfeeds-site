Before you start

Three things to have at hand. No code anywhere in this guide.

  • An AWIN publisher accountThe one you log in to when you look at your programmes.
  • A shop that has accepted youShops share their feed with affiliates they have approved. A shop you have just joined can take a little while to show up.
  • MyFeeds in your WordPressThe free plugin is enough for one shop. Not installed yet? Follow the install guide first.

Step 1 of 2

Copy the feed link in AWIN

GoalA web address for the shop's product list, copied and ready to paste. About 5 minutes

  1. Open the place for product feeds

    In your AWIN account, look for the area where you choose shops' product feeds and get a download address for them. AWIN calls it Create-a-Feed.

    Menus move. If it is not where you expect it, search AWIN's help centre for “Create-a-Feed”.

  2. Pick the shop and its feed

    Choose the shop you want. Most shops have one feed, some have one for each part of their range. Leave all columns selected: MyFeeds finds the ones it needs.

  3. Copy the address

    AWIN builds a web address for what you chose. Copy all of it, from https to the last character.

    What you copied looks roughly like this

    https://…/datafeed/download/apikey/YOUR-KEY/fid/12345/format/csv/…

    • YOUR-KEY is your personal key. Treat the whole address like a password.
    • 12345 stands for the number of the shop's feed. Another shop, another number.

    A shortened example. Your address is longer.

You have the link copied. On to step 2

Step 2 of 2

Add it in WordPress

GoalThe shop's products are in your WordPress, ready to search. 1 minute, then the import runs on its own
Recommended

Paste the link

  1. Click MyFeeds in the WordPress sidebar, then Add your first feed.
  2. Give the feed the shop's name. Readers see it on your cards.
  3. Under Where is the feed? keep At a URL, paste the address and click Create Feed.
  • MyFeeds fetches a link again every night, so prices and stock stay right.
If you only have a file

Upload the file

  1. Download the shop's feed from AWIN as a file.
  2. In the same dialog, choose Upload a file and select it.
  3. Click Create Feed.
  • A file does not update by itself. When the shop's prices change, upload it again.

Let it import. The import runs in the background. A feed with 10,000 products typically takes two to five minutes, and you can keep working meanwhile.

The shop's products are in. Publish your first cards

What the feed contains

AWIN's classic format names its columns like this. You do not have to match anything by hand.

Columns of an AWIN feed and what a card shows from them
Column in the feedWhat your card shows
product_nameThe product's name
brand_nameThe brand, above the name
search_priceThe price
merchant_image_urlThe picture
aw_deep_linkWhere a tap leads: the product in the shop, through your affiliate link
merchant_nameThe shop's name, at the bottom of the card

No settings to work out. MyFeeds recognises the format on its own and finds which column is the title, the price, the picture and the link. If a shop names a column in a way MyFeeds has never seen, the mapping editor lets you point it at the right field.

A column is empty? Then the shop has not filled it in. MyFeeds cannot show what the feed does not carry.

Questions about AWIN feeds

The snags people run into most. Still stuck? Write to us.

The shop I joined does not show up

A shop you have just joined can take up to an hour to appear. If it is still missing after that, check whether the shop offers a product feed at all. Not every shop does.

Do I need a new link for every shop?

Yes. Each shop has its own feed and its own link. The free plugin holds one shop's feed, every paid plan holds as many as you like.

MyFeeds says the link returned a web page

Then the address is not the feed itself. Copy it again in AWIN, the whole address, and check that it opens without a login.

Prices on my cards do not change

Check whether the feed was added as an uploaded file. A file stays as it was the day you uploaded it. Add the feed again with At a URL and the link from AWIN, and MyFeeds fetches it every night.
