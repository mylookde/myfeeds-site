How Amazon links work

“Where did you get that?”

Your photo is why readers want what's in it. Then they have to find it in your post.

  • A list of links under the photo. Your reader works out which link is which.
  • A widget from someone else's platform. Collective Voice, once ShopStyle Collective, has closed.
  • A price typed by hand. It was right on the day you published.

Now the photo answers.

A tap opens a small card: the name, the store's current price, your note and the way to the store.

  • The list under the photo is easy to tap on a phone.
  • The look is part of your post, not a widget from a platform.
  • When a store's product sells out or is gone, its dot leaves the photo on its own.

Four steps in the WordPress block editor.

For dots with store products, MyFeeds needs each store's product list. Most stores in an affiliate network offer a list of all their products with picture, price and your affiliate link. It's called a product feed. See which networks. Amazon dots need no feed.

  1. Choose a photo

    Pick one from your media library: your own, or one you have the right to use.

  2. Click on the photo

    Each click sets a numbered dot, up to 30. Drag to move it.

  3. floor lamp

    Fill one field per dot

    Type a product's name to search the product feeds you added, or paste the product's link from one of those stores or from Amazon.

  4. Publish

    Readers reach each store through your affiliate link.

Amazon links on your own photo.

MyFeeds is a WordPress plugin. An Amazon dot is a link with your tag (your Amazon tracking ID), without picture and without price.

  • Type a name and add the product. In SiteStripe, choose the full link, not the amzn.to short link, or copy the ASIN, Amazon's product number.
  • The dot shows the name you typed, your note and a link straight to the Amazon site your tag belongs to.
  • You need your tag from Amazon Associates, Amazon's affiliate program, nothing else from Amazon.
  • The dot never shows Amazon's photo or price. Amazon's rules don't let a site store its photos, or show a price Amazon did not supply.
  • MyFeeds cannot see when an Amazon product is gone. Check those dots now and then.

Questions before you start

Can I try it first?

Yes, with the 7-day Starter trial. The checkout asks for a card or PayPal. Cancel from your dashboard before the trial ends and you pay nothing. The free plugin has product cards (products from one store, shown in your posts), not the look and no Amazon links.

Which plan do I need?

Starter, $9 a month. It does everything on this page: dots with store products and Amazon links. Pro, $19 a month, adds click counts per post and product. Later, once Amazon lets you use its product data (after 10 qualifying sales in 30 days), Pro can also show Amazon photos and prices in product cards, still not on dots.

Does it work with my editor?

Yes, if you write your posts in the standard WordPress editor, the block editor (also called Gutenberg), where every paragraph and picture is its own block. MyFeeds does not work in the Classic editor, and it has no Elementor or Divi widget.

Is this allowed by Amazon and Pinterest?

Amazon's program is made for links on your own site. The disclosures stay your job: MyFeeds adds none. A Pin that leads to your post carries no affiliate link itself; our guide to affiliate links on Pinterest has the rest. This is not legal advice.
