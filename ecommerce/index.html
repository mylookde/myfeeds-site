Before you start the trial

The shop-specific questions.

What is the storefront exactly?

A real shop page on your domain. Your products appear in a categorised, filterable, searchable layout, sorted by price, popularity or sale. Visitors browse the way they would on any retailer's site, and every checkout goes through your affiliate link.

Do I have to sort products into categories by hand?

The smart mapper suggests products by keyword, brand and feed source. You confirm the matches, refine the rules, and later imports drop into the right categories on their own.

Does the shop affect my blog?

No. The shop lives on its own URL and you pick the slug. Your posts stay untouched. The picker that fills your posts is the same one that fills the shop: one catalogue, two surfaces.

Can I design the storefront myself?

Colours, typography, tile density, hover behaviour, badge placement, sidebar position and pagination mode are all editable with a live preview. No code.

What can I sell?

Anything your affiliate networks expose in a feed: fashion, tech, home, beauty, garden, hobbies. The plugin doesn't care about the niche. If your network hands you a feed URL, you can stand up a shop.

How long is the trial?

14 days with full access to the shop, the design editor, the SEO engine and smart categories. Cancel from your dashboard whenever. Your free-plan setup and the posts you have built stay intact.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

The storefront stops being publicly accessible. Your products, feeds, posts and the free-plan picker keep working. Re-subscribing brings the shop back the way you left it.
