Feed link guides · Sovrn Commerce

Sovrn Commerce has no product feed.

Sovrn turns the ordinary links in your posts into affiliate links. It does not hand out a list of a shop's products, so there is nothing from Sovrn to import into WordPress.

What Sovrn gives you, and what it leaves out

Product cards with picture and price are built from a product feed: the full list of what one shop sells. Sovrn works differently.

  • Links that earnSovrn's script turns the plain shop links on your pages into affiliate links. Single links you build yourself, in the tab Sovrn currently calls Create links.
  • One review instead of manySovrn reviews your site once, which can take five business days. After that you don't apply to each shop.
  • Its own product widgetsPrice Comparisons shows one product at several shops, as a table, a list or text. Shopping Galleries is a carousel of products you curate.

Not on the list: a shop's whole range. Sovrn's developer hub lists tools for links, reports, approved shops and product lookups. A download of everything one shop sells is not among them (as of October 2026).

The way that works

Product cards need a feed from the network the shop is on.

A shop usually runs its affiliate programme through a network. If the shop shares a product feed, that network passes it on to the affiliates the shop has accepted.

  1. You need an account there

    With the network the shop is on, and the shop has to accept you. Sovrn spared you that step. A feed does not.

  2. That network pays you

    Each product in the feed carries your link for that network. The sales appear in its reports, not in Sovrn's.

  3. Not every shop has a feed

    For a shop that shares none, a Sovrn link in your text stays the way to earn.

Three steps to a shop's feed

None of them needs our plugin. The feed is yours to use with any tool that reads one.

  1. Find the network the shop is on

    Look on the shop's site for a link named Affiliates or Partner programme, often in the footer. That page usually names the network that runs the programme.

  2. Join the shop's programme there

    Open an account with that network and apply to the shop.

  3. Get the feed from that network

    Each network hands a shop's products over in its own way: as a link, as a file, or through an interface. Our guides say which it is and where to find it.

Questions about Sovrn and product cards

For anything else, write to us.

Is a feed worth it when Sovrn already covers the shop?

Only for a shop you show often. A feed pays off when you want many of one shop's products in your posts, with picture and price.

For a shop you mention twice a year, a Sovrn link is less work, and it earns without a second account. For a row of products without that account, look at Sovrn's Shopping Galleries first.

Can I use a shop's own product file, without affiliate links?

It usually imports, but the cards then carry the shop's plain links, and a plain link earns nothing.

MyFeeds does not turn plain links into Sovrn links, and we could not confirm that Sovrn's script does so inside a product card. Don't build a post on it.

Can MyFeeds use Sovrn's product APIs instead?

No. MyFeeds does not connect to them, and they answer one request at a time. Price Comparisons takes a barcode, a link or keywords and returns the best offer from each shop. Product Recommendation takes a text and returns up to 50 matching products.

A feed is the full list of one shop's products, and you pick your cards from that list.

More questions

Will Sovrn's script change the links in my product cards?

We could not confirm it either way. Sovrn's help says the script converts plain shop links, and the links in a network's feed are affiliate links already.

If you run both on one page, click a card after publishing and check in that network's reports that the click arrived.

Can I see in Sovrn which link earned?

Yes, with a CUID, Sovrn's name for a tracking ID of your own. Enter it under Tracking when you create a link, or add it to Sovrn's script.

For the results, request the CUID/UTM report in the Analytics tab. Sovrn emails it to you.

Does MyFeeds count clicks on Sovrn links?

ProFrom the Pro plan, MyFeeds counts the clicks on your product cards, by post and product. Links in your text that Sovrn converts are not cards. Their clicks are in Sovrn's reports.
