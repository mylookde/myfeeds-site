Before you start

A feed pays off when you want many products from one shop, with picture and price. For two or three, a normal CJ link in your text is enough. How a shop's product feed gets into WordPress explains the idea.

  • You have joined the shopOnly joined publishers can subscribe to a shop's full feed. CJ's word for a shop is advertiser.
  • The shop shares its feed with youNot every shop keeps a feed at CJ, and some limit who gets it.
  • MyFeeds is in your WordPressNot installed yet? The install guide takes you through it.

Step 1 of 2

Get the file from CJ

GoalThe shop's product feed as a file on your computer.

  1. Find your subscriptions

    Look for the part of your CJ account that sends you a shop's product feed. CJ's own pages list it under Account, as Subscriptions.

    We could not read CJ's walkthrough for publishers. It sits behind your login, so a button may be named differently in your account: CJ support.

  2. Subscribe to the shop's feed

    Create a product export for the shop you joined. If the shop keeps several feeds, each for one country, currency and language, take the country most of your readers shop in.

  3. Choose where CJ sends it

    CJ's blog names two destinations: your inbox, or an FTP service, a file server you log in to. Your account may list more. MyFeeds cannot log in anywhere, so you need the file on your computer.

  4. Read one link before you upload

    It may not be your tracking link. In CJ's feed format, the column link is the shop's own address, and a sale through it is not credited to you. We could not confirm which link CJ's export carries, so look at one row of the file. If it holds the shop's own address, ask CJ how to get your tracking links before you publish cards.

    Two kinds of link a row can hold

    https://www.example-shop.com/court-high-top/
    https://www.kqzyfj.com/…

    • example-shop.com stands for the shop's own address. Not tracked.
    • kqzyfj.com is the address in CJ's own example. Tracked. Yours may differ.

    Shortened examples.

You have the file. On to step 2

Step 2 of 2

Upload the file in WordPress

The first time

Upload the file

  1. In WordPress, open MyFeeds and click Add your first feed.
  2. Name the feed after the shop: readers see that name on your cards. Choose Upload a file and select CJ's file.
  3. Click Create Feed and let it import in the background. The feed's row shows the number of products.
  • Prices and stock freeze on upload day, until you upload a newer file.
When CJ sends a newer file

Upload it again

  1. On the feed's row, click Edit and select the newer file.
  2. Click Save Changes, then Reimport.
  • Your published cards follow the newer file. You don't edit the posts.

Check the first import. We have not tested a current CJ export from end to end. Search for a product in the editor and check picture, price and link. If a column landed in the wrong place, correct it in the Mapping Editor.

What changes at CJ

A shop sends CJ its products whenever it likes, or CJ collects them once a day. CJ says you may get a new copy each time.

If the shop is on another network too

AWIN and Partnerize, among others, hand out a link. MyFeeds reads a link again every night, so the uploads fall away.

Questions about CJ feeds

For anything else, write to us.

I joined the shop. Why can't I subscribe to its feed?

Either the shop keeps no product feed at CJ, or it has limited who may export it.

Ask the shop, or your contact at CJ, to open the feed for you.

Can my cards update without a new upload?

Only if each new file lands at an https address that opens without a login.

MyFeeds reads such an address every night: choose At a URL. Getting each file there takes a script on your own server, outside the plugin. CJ's product API is closed to MyFeeds too: it asks for a personal token.

More questions

The file is a .zip or a .gz. Can I upload it like that?

Yes. MyFeeds opens both by itself, and reads CSV, tab- or pipe-separated text and XML. An upload can be up to 500 MB, or less if your server allows less.

Why is a sold-out product still in the file?

CJ keeps sold-out products in its exports. Only the availability field changes (as of October 2026).

Can I see which post and product a click came from?

ProFrom the Pro plan, MyFeeds counts every click on a card, by post and product. What a click earned is in your CJ reports.
