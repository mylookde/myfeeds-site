Group 2 of 11 · .chap

The reading column

This band is a .chap: from 1000px the head stays here while the text scrolls. The right side is a .col: text blocks in .prose, wider things between them.

.proseRunning text in one measure (58ch, about 84 letters a line), 17px, 18px from 900px. Plain ul and ol inside it are styled by themselves.

Body text sits in a column of its own, narrow enough to read without losing the line. A paragraph holds one thought and two to four sentences, then the next one starts.

What the reader clicks in WordPress is marked like Add New Plugin, what they type like /myfeeds. A link in the text looks like this, and a strong phrase like this.

A heading inside the text

After a heading comes a short paragraph, never a list straight away.

  • A plain list in the text gets ticks by itself.
  • Each item is one sentence, not a paragraph.
  1. A numbered list gets small circles.
  2. Use it when the order matters.

Lists and steps

Lists are open, not boxed: ticks, or numbers in circles.

.stepsThe short version of a process: three bold openings, three columns on a desktop.

  1. Paste a shop's product feed link. You get it from your affiliate network, for each shop you work with. See which networks.
  2. Pick your products. Search the feed inside the WordPress editor.
  3. Publish. Prices refresh every night from then on.

.needWhat to have at hand before a guide starts.

  • A WordPress site you can log in toAs an administrator, so you can add plugins.
  • WordPress 5.8 or newer, PHP 7.4 or newerAnd the block editor. The Classic Editor is not supported.
  • A shop you're an affiliate forThrough an affiliate network, with a product feed. No account yet? Try it with sample products first.

.subThe steps of a how-to: a heading each, joined by a line. Notes go inside the step.

  1. Open your WordPress admin

    Log in to your WordPress site. You land on the Dashboard, the main admin page with the sidebar on the left.

  2. Go to Plugins, then Add New Plugin

    In the sidebar, hover over Plugins and click Add New Plugin.

    Rather install from a ZIP? Download it from the WordPress.org page and upload it.

  3. Search for MyFeeds, install, activate

    Type MyFeeds into the search box, click Install Now, then Activate.

.stakesProblems or points: a number, a heading, one sentence.

  1. Readers see links, not products

  2. Every product takes minutes by hand

    Copy the link, save the image, retype the price, repeat.

Is a product feed the same as an affiliate link?

No. An affiliate link points to one product. A feed is the list of all of a shop's products, and every row has its own affiliate link in it.

That is why a feed saves the copying: the link for a product is already there the moment you pick it.

Does every shop have a product feed?

No. A shop decides whether it shares one with its affiliates.

Is a product feed the same as an affiliate link?

No. An affiliate link points to one product. A feed is the list of all of a shop's products, and every row has its own affiliate link in it.

That is why a feed saves the copying: the link for a product is already there the moment you pick it.

Does every shop have a product feed?

No. A shop decides whether it shares one with its affiliates.

Can I try this without a feed?

FreeYes. Click Load 7 sample products on the empty MyFeeds screen in WordPress and build a post with them.

How many feeds can I add?

The free plugin holds one shop's feed. Every paid plan holds as many as you like. See the plans.
