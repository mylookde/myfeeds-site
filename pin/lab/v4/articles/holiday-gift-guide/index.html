Guides · Seasonal posts

How to make a holiday gift guide for your blog.

Pick about ten gifts, sort them by person and budget, say at the top that you earn, and keep prices and stock right until Christmas.

You'll leave with a layout to copy, disclosure wording and a checklist for Black Friday week. Start with the 3-step plan.

Is there still time for a gift guide?

Yes. Pinterest says gift searches run until December, when gifts for others surge. Publish now, even in November.

The plan, in three steps

  1. Pick about ten gifts. By person and budget, each section with a backup.
  2. Build it this week. The template, your disclosure on top, first pins.
  3. Keep it right. A Black Friday section, the full check twice.

When should you publish and pin it?

  1. October: Publish, then the first pins.
  2. November: More pins, and Black Friday deals.
  3. Black Friday week: A daily look at prices, deals and stock until Cyber Monday.
  4. December: Swap in backups, keep pinning.
  5. January: Keep the address for next year.

Step 1 of 3

Which gifts go in, and how many?

About ten, and only ones you would recommend without the commission. Five to 15 is usual; Matt McWilliams puts the sweet spot at eight to ten.

For the sections, search Pinterest for christmas gift ideas and keep the people it suggests, such as mom or coworker. Add a budget, and fill them from stores you're already an affiliate for. New to this? Start with how a blog earns.

Give every section a backup. Some gifts sell out before Christmas, so note a second one for each section, at about the same price:

  • For the cook: a carbon-steel pan, and a similar pan from a second store.
  • For the gardener: leather work gloves, and a pair from another brand.
  • Under $25: pocket notebooks, and another set at the same price.

Step 2 of 3: the disclosure

How do you say that you earn from it?

At the top, in plain words, before the first link.

The FTC warns that “affiliate link” alone may not tell readers you are paid, and a line at the bottom is easy to miss. In the UK, put “Ad” in the title. Pins need their own line: our Pinterest guide has the rules.

At the top of the post

I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post.

At the start of a pin

Ad: I earn a commission if you buy through this link.

On your site, if you link to Amazon

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

The first line is the FTC's example, the last is Amazon's own. Elsewhere, check with your consumer authority. As of October 2026, not legal advice.

What about gifts from Amazon?

  • Welcome, with your own Amazon affiliate link and your own words.
  • No price you type yourself: only links and data from Amazon may show one.
  • No Amazon pictures saved, or in a collage.
  • No “Black Friday deal” once the deal ends.
  • No printed guide or PDF with Amazon links.

Amazon leaves open whether “Under $25” counts as showing a price, so sort Amazon gifts by person only.

Does Amazon allow gift guide collages? Not from its pictures, in our reading. Take one photo of your own: Shop the look in MyFeeds lets you link each gift with a numbered dot.

Step 3 of 3

How do you keep it right until Christmas?

Two full checks and a daily look in Black Friday week.

What changes in Black Friday week?

Prices, deals and stock. When the UK consumer group Which? tracked 208 deals, only five were cheapest on Black Friday itself.

Questions people ask

Can I include gifts I haven't tried?

Yes, as long as you don't claim you have. The FTC says you can't talk about your experience with a product you haven't tried, and Awin calls gift guides suggestions, not necessarily recommendations. So write “a good pick for a new runner”, and “I use this every day” only where it is true.

Should I use the same URL every year?

Yes. Keep one address and update the page each fall. Put the year in the title if you like, never in the address, so pins from past seasons lead to this year's guide.

Does MyFeeds work with my blog?

Yes, if it runs on WordPress with the block editor. You also need the product feed of a store that has accepted you as an affiliate, as a link from your network: a file you upload keeps the prices of the day you uploaded it. No product feed yet? Can I show Amazon gifts as cards?

Yes, in the paid plans, with your Amazon tracking ID. The card shows the name you give it, your note and a link to Amazon, without picture or price, so there is no price to go out of date. It is not checked for stock.
