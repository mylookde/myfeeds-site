Before you start

A shop's product feed pays off when you show many of its products, with picture and price. For two or three, a normal AWIN link in your text is enough.

  • The shop has approved youAWIN opens a shop's feed once the shop has accepted you.
  • The shop shares a product feedNot every shop does. AWIN currently shows it under Advertisers (its word for shops), then My Programmes, in the column Product Feed. With No, there is nothing to import.
  • MyFeeds is in your WordPressOur plugin for the WordPress block editor. The install guide takes you through setting it up.

Step 1 of 2

Get the feed link in AWIN

GoalOne link to the shop's product feed, ready to paste, whatever you paste it into.

  1. Open AWIN's feed tool

    It makes a download link for a shop's product feed (AWIN says data feed). AWIN currently calls it Create-a-Feed and keeps it in the Toolbox menu.

    Menus move. If it is gone, see AWIN's own walkthrough: Downloading feeds using Create-a-Feed.

  2. Choose the language, then the shop

    Set the feed language first: AWIN then lists only feeds in that language. Any lists them all.

    Then pick the shop. Most have one feed, called Default. If a shop has several, take the one with the products you write about.

    Where a shop offers two formats, take AWIN's own, listed as Legacy.

  3. Select all columns

    On the next screen, select every column. One button does it.

    Your tracking link is one of them. It is called aw_deep_link. Without it, cards carry the shop's plain link: a reader can buy and you get no commission, and no error tells you.

  4. Copy the link

    AWIN asks for a compression method: gzip works.

    AWIN then offers a CSV file to download or a URL to copy. Copy the whole URL: AWIN calls it a reusable download link.

    What you copied looks roughly like this

    https://…/datafeed/download/apikey/YOUR-KEY/fid/12345/format/csv/language/en/…/compression/gzip/…/columns/aw_deep_link%2Cproduct_name%2C…/

    • YOUR-KEY is personal. Treat the whole link like a password: keep it out of screenshots and forum posts.
    • aw_deep_link has to appear after columns. Check before you paste.

    An invented, shortened example.

You have the link copied. On to step 2

Step 2 of 2

Add the link in WordPress

GoalThe shop's products in WordPress, ready to search in the editor.
Recommended

Paste the link

  1. In WordPress, open MyFeeds and click Add your first feed. On a paid plan, later feeds go through + Add Feed.
  2. Name the feed after the shop: readers see that name on your cards. Keep At a URL, paste the link and click Create Feed.
  3. Let it import in the background while you keep working. When it is done, the feed's row shows the number of products.
  • MyFeeds reads a link again every night.
If MyFeeds shows an error for the link

Upload the file

  1. In AWIN, download the feed as a CSV file.
  2. In the same MyFeeds dialog, choose Upload a file.
  • A file stays as it was on upload day. Prices and stock freeze until you upload a newer one.

Send us the error message too: support@myfeeds.site.

The shop's products are in. Publish your first cards

How your cards stay current

Every night

Price, crossed-out price, stock, picture and link of the products in your published posts.

Once a week

The whole feed. New products become searchable. A product the shop has removed disappears from your posts.

One limit. Shops on AWIN often update their feed several times a day, so a card can lag until the next nightly read.

Questions about AWIN feeds

For anything else, write to us.

Why is the shop I joined missing from the list?

Wait an hour after the shop approved you, then set the feed language to Any.

If it is still missing although Product Feed says Yes, AWIN's advice is to ask the shop or your account manager.

How do I know my cards carry my tracking link?

The link you pasted has to list aw_deep_link after columns.

If not, copy a new link with that column selected. In MyFeeds, click Edit on the feed's row, swap the link and click Save Changes. Then click Reimport.

More questions

The link worked. Now the import fails.

Wait an hour, then click Reimport once.

A likely cause is AWIN's download limit: five downloads at once, and five identical requests within an hour (as of October 2026).

The shop's feed says Enhanced. Does that work?

We could not test it: Enhanced is the shop's feed in Google's layout.

If you try it, select aw_deep_link there too. In that format, link is the shop's plain link. MyFeeds uses aw_deep_link when a row has it.

Can I see which post and product a click came from?

ProFrom the Pro plan, yes: clicks on your cards are counted by post and product. Click Edit on the feed's row, set Network Hint to AWIN under Advanced Options and save. Your affiliate links then also get a click ID in clickref, the field AWIN reports back.

A limit on AWIN's side: each click gets its own ID, and AWIN disables its Click References report for 30 days above 20,000 different ones (as of October 2026).
