How it works

From link list to shop in three steps.

  1. Product feed · Northside Supply

    • Grey Hoodie$58.00your link
    • Boxy Heavy Tee$32.00your link
    • and every other product in the store

    Add a store's product list

    Most stores in an affiliate network offer a list of all their products with picture, price and your affiliate link. It's called a product feed. Paste its link into MyFeeds, or upload the file.

    Find the feed in your network

  2. Search and pick products in the WordPress editor

    Type a word, tick the products you want, and they go into your post as cards.

  3. Publish. The prices follow the store.

    Every night MyFeeds reads each store's feed link again and updates price and stock in your posts.

No feed yet? Try it with 7 sample products. They come with the free plugin.

Questions before you start

Does it work with my Amazon links?

Yes, from Starter: paste the full Amazon address of a product (not an amzn.to link) and give it a name. The card shows that name and a button to Amazon with your own Associates tag, without picture or price. Pro adds Amazon's picture and price to your cards once Amazon opens that access to you, which takes ten qualified sales in thirty days.

Does it work with my network?

Yes, if your network gives you a store's product feed. The network guides show how: a feed link refreshes every night, an uploaded file only when you upload it again. Network missing? Write to support@myfeeds.site.

Do I need tech skills?

No. If you can install a plugin and paste a link, you can set it up. It works in the standard WordPress editor, the one with blocks.

What does it cost to start?

Nothing: the free plugin on WordPress.org covers the products of one store. Paid plans start at $9 a month with a free trial and come as MyFeeds Pro, which replaces the free plugin and picks up everything it saved.

What happens after the trial?

Every trial asks for a card or PayPal. Cancel on the Manage Plan page in your WordPress admin before it ends and you pay nothing. If you don't cancel, the plan continues at the price you chose, with a 14-day money-back guarantee if a bug stops MyFeeds' main features from working and we can't fix it. When a plan ends, MyFeeds falls back to the free features, and your posts and settings stay as they are.

Will it slow my blog down?

The cards are built from products stored in your own WordPress, so your posts don't wait on a product service to answer. The one exception is Amazon with picture and price on Pro, which comes live from Amazon.
