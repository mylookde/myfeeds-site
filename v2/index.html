So what is this, exactly?

Affiliate networks hand every publisher a product feed: one file listing everything a merchant sells, with prices, images, stock and your tracking links already in it. Almost nobody uses it, because on its own it is a spreadsheet with a hundred thousand rows.

MyFeeds is the WordPress plugin that turns that file into something a reader wants to look at. It imports the feed into your own database, gives you a search box inside the post editor, and renders whatever you pick as product cards styled to match your site. On the E-commerce tier the same catalogue becomes a browsable storefront on your domain.

What happens after the first import →

The plugin re-reads your feed every night and updates the cards that are already live in your posts, so prices and stock stay right without you opening anything. Products a merchant has removed get flagged, with a list of the posts still pointing at them. A weekly full import catches everything the nightly pass doesn't.

Everything lives in your own WordPress database. No product images are proxied, no third-party server is contacted when a page loads, and nothing about your visitors leaves your site.

07

Common questions

Quick answers before you install.

Does this work with my affiliate network?

If your network gives you a product feed URL, MyFeeds reads it. AWIN, CJ, TradeDoubler, Impact, Rakuten, Webgains, Admitad, Pepperjam, FlexOffers and Sovrn are recognised out of the box. Generic CSV, TSV, XML and JSON feeds work too — the smart mapper figures out which column is which on its own.

What if my feed format is unusual?

It still works. MyFeeds recognises the format on its own, and when a merchant leaves a column out or empty, it looks for the same information elsewhere in the row instead of giving up. A messy feed doesn't break your import.

Do I have to pay to get started?

No. The free version lives on WordPress.org and covers one feed, the smart search, the grid layout, manual plus nightly sync. Upgrade only when you outgrow it.

What happens after the trial?

The Pro trial is 7 days, the Business trial is 14 days. If you don't continue, your plugin falls back to the free tier. Your existing feeds, posts and configuration stay intact.

How long does setup take?

Most setups land in five to ten minutes. Paste your feed URL, MyFeeds imports and maps it. Open the block editor, type two letters, drop a card in. That's the loop.

Where does my data live?

On your own WordPress site, in your own database. The plugin doesn't proxy product images, doesn't ping a third-party server when a page loads, and doesn't send anything about your visitors anywhere.

Do I need technical skills?

No code, no command line, no terminal. If you can install a WordPress plugin and paste a URL, you're done.

What happens when a merchant removes a product?

Nightly sync flags it. The plugin tells you which posts still link to it, and you replace it with a working alternative. Old posts stop quietly pointing at 404s.

Networks we already speak fluently

If your network isn't on this list but it hands you a product feed URL, it almost certainly works too. MyFeeds reads the most common feed formats and figures out the field structure on its own.
