This is a sample product

Nothing went wrong. You clicked a demo card.

The product you clicked is part of the sample data that ships with the MyFeeds WordPress plugin. The brand, the price and the discount are invented, and there is no merchant behind them. Every demo card leads here.

What a real card does instead

On a live site, this click would have gone straight to the merchant, through the affiliate link that came out of the merchant's own product feed. The price on the card would be the price in that feed, refreshed every night, so it matches what the shop is charging today.

That is the whole point of the plugin: the card is not typed out by hand, so it does not go stale, and the link does not quietly die when a product is discontinued.

If this is your own site

You loaded the samples from the MyFeeds screen in your WordPress admin, and you can remove them there in one click — the Feeds screen has a Remove demo products button. Do that once your own feed is set up, and remember to unpublish or delete any post you used them in.

About MyFeeds

MyFeeds turns an affiliate product feed into searchable product cards inside the WordPress block editor. It is free on the WordPress plugin directory, and there are paid plans for people who want a carousel, their own card design, earnings analytics or a full storefront.
