What each plan does.

Most stores in an affiliate network offer a list of all their products with picture, price and your affiliate link. It's called a product feed.

You add a store by pasting its feed link from your network. A downloaded file works too, but only a link refreshes every night. The install guide shows how.

Questions about the plans

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. To move from the free plugin to a paid plan, you install the paid plugin in its place. It is called MyFeeds Pro and serves Starter, Pro and E-commerce. It keeps your stores, posts and settings. Between paid plans, you switch from inside the plugin, in WordPress. The install guide shows each step.

How do I cancel?

Any time, from your WordPress dashboard, or by writing to support@myfeeds.site. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. Once a paid period has started, the plan runs until that period ends.

Can I get a refund?

Yes: if a bug stops MyFeeds' main features from working in the 14 days after your first payment and we can't fix it, you get the full amount back. Missing features and conflicts with other plugins, themes or hosting are not covered; the refund policy has the details.

Which plan do I need for Amazon?

Starter. Paste the product's full address from Amazon, not a short link like amzn.to, and give it a name. The card shows that name and a button to Amazon, with your Amazon affiliate tag in the link. It shows no picture or price.

Pro adds Amazon's picture and price to your cards once you have 10 qualified Amazon sales in 30 days. That is Amazon's rule, and Amazon can change it. Pro counts your Amazon clicks; your Amazon earnings stay in your Amazon account.

I run several sites. What does that cost?

Each plan covers one site. Pro and E-commerce also come for three sites: Pro $39 a month or $399 a year, E-commerce $89 a month or $899 a year. Starter is for one site. The free plugin goes on as many sites as you like.

Does the free plugin stay free?

Yes. It lives on WordPress.org, costs nothing and has no time limit. It sets no limit on products or posts, and nothing in it stops working. It reads one store's feed; more stores, your own design and Shop the look come with Starter.
