MyFeeds is now available on WordPress.org

May 25, 2026

MyFeeds is live in the WordPress plugin directory.

The problem it solves. Affiliate posts decay quietly. Products go out of stock, prices drift away from what the merchant is charging, and merchants pull feeds without notice. A roundup that converted well in its first month can spend the next year sending readers to dead pages, and nothing in WordPress tells you it is happening. The damage is invisible in your dashboard: fewer clicks, no error, no alert.

The usual answer is to go back and check every post by hand every few months. That is workable at ten posts. It stops being workable at fifty.

MyFeeds takes a different route. You connect your affiliate network's product feed once. Every product that network sells becomes searchable inside the WordPress block editor, so you pick the ones you want and place them as cards without copying a single link. From then on the plugin re-reads the feed every night: prices stay current, sold-out products are flagged, and when a product disappears entirely you get a list of every post that still points at it.

What is available:

  • Free on WordPress.org — 1 feed, smart search, nightly sync, and the common feed formats read automatically
  • Starter at $9/mo — unlimited feeds, carousel layout, card design editor, Google Fonts
  • Pro at $19/mo — everything in Starter plus the analytics dashboard, dead-product detection and conversion sync
  • E-commerce at $49/mo — a full affiliate storefront on your own domain

Updated August 2026: the plans were restructured after launch. The prices above are the current ones, not what shipped on day one.
