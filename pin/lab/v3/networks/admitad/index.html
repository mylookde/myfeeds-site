Before you start

A shop's product feed pays off when you show many of its products, with picture and price. For two or three, a link from Admitad's Deeplink Generator is enough.

  • An ad space for your blogAdmitad's name for the site your links appear on. You cannot join a programme without one.
  • A shop that has a feedOnly programmes in Admitad's Online stores category have feeds, and only if the shop supplied one. Join the programme with your ad space first.
  • MyFeeds in your WordPressOur plugin for the block editor. The install guide takes you through it.

Step 1 of 2

Generate the feed link in Admitad

GoalOne link to the shop's product feed, ready to paste.

  1. Open Admitad's feed form

    It turns a shop's product feed into a download link. Admitad currently calls this part of your account Product Feeds and keeps the form under Original.

    Menus move. If it is gone, Admitad's own walkthrough shows where it is now.

  2. Fill in the form

    Leave the last two alone. Each shortens the feed: to sale items, or to changes since a date. MyFeeds treats a product missing from the feed as removed, and its card leaves your posts.

  3. Generate, then copy the link

    Click Generate. The link appears in the field Generated Link. Copy all of it.

You have the link copied. On to step 2

Step 2 of 2

Add the link in WordPress

GoalThe shop's products in WordPress, ready to search in the editor.
Try this first

Paste the link

  1. In WordPress, open MyFeeds and click Add your first feed.
  2. Name the feed after the shop: readers see that name on your cards. Keep At a URL, paste the link and click Create Feed.
  3. Let it import. When it is done, the feed's row shows the number of products.
  • MyFeeds reads a link again every night.
  • Admitad does not say how long a generated link keeps working.
If MyFeeds shows an error for the link

Upload the file

  1. Paste the generated link into your browser. Admitad sends the feed as a file.
  2. In the same MyFeeds dialog, choose Upload a file.
  • A file stays as it was on upload day. Prices and stock freeze until you upload a newer one.

Send us the error message, and we help you get the link working: support@myfeeds.site.

The shop's products are in. Publish your first cards

What Admitad's feed gives you

One row for each product, with the link that pays you already in it.

Every night, MyFeeds reads the link again and updates price, crossed-out price, stock, picture and link of the products in your published posts. Once a week it reads the whole feed, and new products become searchable.

Questions about Admitad feeds

For anything else, write to us.

Does the generated link keep working?

Admitad's help does not say, and we could not confirm it: MyFeeds is not yet tested against a live Admitad account.

If the feed's row in MyFeeds shows an error, generate a fresh link, click Edit on that row, swap the link, click Save Changes and then Reimport. If that fails too, upload the file.

The import fails on a large shop

The download probably took too long. Admitad says a large feed can take a few minutes, and MyFeeds stops waiting after two.

Take a smaller feed if the shop offers several, or download the file in your browser and upload it.

More questions

CSV, XML or Google Merchant: which format?

CSV or XML: MyFeeds knows Admitad's column names in both. Google Merchant is Google's own layout, which MyFeeds reads too.

Look at your first cards. If a picture or a price is missing, the Mapping Editor in MyFeeds lets you point that field at the right column.

Can I see which sales came from my cards?

In Admitad, yes, with a SubID: a label of your own. Under Product Feeds, open Export templates, add a template with a SubID such as blog, and choose it in the form before you generate.

Admitad puts the label on all product links in the feed and shows it under Reports, on SubID. MyFeeds leaves the links as they are.

ProFrom the Pro plan, MyFeeds also counts the clicks on your cards, by post and product. What a click earned stays in Admitad's reports.
