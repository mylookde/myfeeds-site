Before you start

A feed pays off when you want to show many products from one shop, with picture and price. For two or three products, a normal AWIN link in your text is enough. How a shop's product feed gets into WordPress explains the idea.

  • The shop has approved youAWIN opens a shop's feed once the shop has accepted you. A fresh approval can take up to an hour to show.
  • The shop shares a product feedNot every shop does. AWIN currently shows it under Advertisers (AWIN's word for shops), then My Programmes, in the column Product Feed. With No, there is nothing to import. You can still link to that shop by hand.
  • MyFeeds is in your WordPressOur plugin for the WordPress block editor. If it is not installed yet, the install guide takes you through it.

Step 1 of 2

Get the feed link in AWIN

GoalOne link to the shop's product feed, ready to paste. This step is the same whatever you paste the link into.

  1. Open AWIN's feed tool

    It makes a download link for a shop's product feed, which AWIN calls a data feed. AWIN currently names the tool Create-a-Feed and keeps it in the Toolbox menu, under Links & Tools.

    Menus move. If it is gone, see AWIN's own walkthrough: Downloading feeds using Create-a-Feed.

  2. Choose the language, then the shop

    Set the feed language first, to the language your readers read. AWIN then lists only feeds in that language. Any lists them all.

    Then pick the shop. Most have one feed, called Default. If a shop has several, take the one with the products you write about.

    Where a shop offers two formats, take AWIN's own. Its help calls it Legacy or Awin feed. Legacy sounds old, but it is the standard.

  3. Select all columns

    On the next screen, select every column. One button does it.

    Your tracking link is one of them. It is called aw_deep_link. Without it, only the shop's plain link is left. A reader can buy through that and you get no commission, and no error tells you.

  4. Copy the link

    When AWIN asks for a compression method, choose gzip. It only makes the download smaller.

    AWIN then offers the feed as a CSV file to download or as a link to copy (AWIN says URL). Copy the whole link. AWIN calls it a reusable download link.

    What you copied looks roughly like this

    https://…/datafeed/download/apikey/YOUR-KEY/fid/12345/format/csv/language/en/…/compression/gzip/…/columns/aw_deep_link%2Cproduct_name%2C…/

    • YOUR-KEY is your personal feed key. AWIN fills it in. Treat the whole link like a password: keep it out of screenshots and forum posts.
    • 12345 is the number of the shop's feed.
    • aw_deep_link has to appear after columns. Check it once before you paste.

    An invented, shortened example. Yours is longer.

You have the link copied. On to step 2

Step 2 of 2

Add the link in WordPress

GoalThe shop's products in WordPress, ready to search in the editor.
Recommended

Paste the link

  1. In WordPress, open MyFeeds and click Add your first feed. On a paid plan, later feeds go through + Add Feed.
  2. Name the feed after the shop: readers see that name on your cards. Keep At a URL, paste the link and click Create Feed.
  3. Let it import. It runs in the background while you keep working. When it is done, the feed's row shows the number of products.
  • A link is read again every night, so the cards in your published posts follow the shop's prices.

Pro Under Advanced Options, set Network Hint to AWIN, now or later through Edit. Your affiliate links then get a click ID, in the field AWIN reports back.

If MyFeeds shows an error for the link

Upload the file

  1. In AWIN, download the feed as a CSV file.
  2. In the same MyFeeds dialog, choose Upload a file.
  • A file stays as it was on upload day. Prices and stock freeze until you upload a newer one.

Send us the error message too, and we help you get the link working: support@myfeeds.site.

The shop's products are in. Publish your first cards

How your cards stay current

After the first import, the link is read again without you.

Every night

Price, crossed-out price, stock, picture and link of the products in your published posts.

Once a week

The whole feed. New products become searchable. A product the shop has removed disappears from your posts.

One limit. Shops on AWIN often update their feed several times a day. A card can be behind the shop until the next nightly read.

Questions about AWIN feeds

Where people usually get stuck. For anything else, write to us.

Why is the shop I joined missing from the list?

Wait an hour after the shop approved you, then set the feed language to Any.

Those are the two causes AWIN's FAQ names. If the shop is still missing although Product Feed says Yes, AWIN's advice is to ask the shop or your account manager.

How do I know my cards carry my tracking link?

Read the link you pasted. After columns it has to list aw_deep_link.

If not, copy a new link with that column selected. In MyFeeds, click Edit on the feed's row, swap the link, then click Reimport.

The link worked. Now the import fails.

Wait an hour, then click Reimport once.

A likely cause is AWIN's download limit: five downloads at once, and five identical requests within an hour (as of October 2026). Several reimports in a row use that up.

The shop has a feed for each country. Which one?

The one for the country most of your readers shop in.

Each feed has its own language and usually its own currency, and prices show as the feed gives them. Add two countries of one shop, which takes Starter, and most products appear twice in your search.

Doesn't AWIN have its own WordPress plugin?

It does. AWIN's FAQ says that plugin does not update feeds by itself: you upload the files by hand (as of October 2026).

That is where a link in MyFeeds differs: it is read again every night. Without any plugin, the same FAQ says, a feed takes programming knowledge.

More questions

A feed shows up for a shop I never joined. Does a sale count?

It can. For selected shops that accept affiliates automatically, AWIN credits sales made through the feed even if you have not joined. It calls this soft membership. It covers selected shops only, so joining the programme is the way to be sure.

The shop's feed says Enhanced. Does that work?

We could not confirm it: that format, the shop's feed in Google's layout, is untested with MyFeeds. Take AWIN's own format where a shop offers both.

If you try it, select aw_deep_link there too. In that format, link is the shop's plain link and aw_deep_link is your tracked one. When a row has it, MyFeeds uses it.

Is a very large shop a problem?

Usually not. A bigger feed takes longer to import, and you can keep working while it runs. If you write about a few brands only, choose those brands in Create-a-Feed. The link then holds just their products.

A card has no picture or no price

If you selected all columns, the shop left that column empty in its feed. AWIN's advice is to ask the shop or your account manager.

A card's link does not open the product page

The address a card leads to comes from the shop's feed. If it misses the product page, AWIN's advice is to contact its Partner Success team. The contact link is at the bottom of AWIN's feed FAQ, source 3 below.

Can I see which post and product a click came from?

ProFrom the Pro plan, yes: clicks on your cards are counted by post and product. With Network Hint set to AWIN (step 2), your affiliate links also get a click ID in clickref, the field AWIN reports back.

A limit on AWIN's side: each click gets its own ID, and AWIN disables its Click References report for 30 days above 20,000 different ones (as of October 2026).
