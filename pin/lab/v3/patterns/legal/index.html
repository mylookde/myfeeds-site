A legal page

Sample text. This page shows the layout for legal texts. It is not a legal document. The real wording of a policy is never rewritten for style, only given this structure.

1. Who is responsible

A legal page opens with who stands behind it. The contact block is a card, so it is easy to find again and easy to copy.

Responsible for this site MyFeeds
[Name of the operator]
[Street and number]
[Postcode and city], Germany
support@myfeeds.site

2. What this page covers

Each numbered section answers one question a reader may arrive with. The heading says the question in plain words, the first paragraph answers it.

Paragraphs stay short. One thought, then a new paragraph. Nobody reads a legal text for pleasure, so the layout should at least let them find their place.

3. What we collect, and why

Longer sections are split with smaller headings, so a reader can skip to the part that concerns them.

3.1 When you visit

A sub-section starts with its number. That keeps references possible: “see section 3.1”.

3.2 When you write to us

Lists are for things that really are a list:

  • one item with one fact,
  • a second item of the same kind,
  • and a third, not a paragraph in disguise.

4. Your rights

Where a section asks the reader to do something, the way to do it stands right there, as a link in the sentence: support@myfeeds.site.

Use the warning sparingly. A legal page has at most one, for the single thing a reader must not miss.

5. Changes and contact

The last section says how changes are announced. The date at the top of the page is the date of the last change. Legal pages are the only pages on this site that carry a date.

Questions about this page?

Write to support@myfeeds.site and a person answers.
