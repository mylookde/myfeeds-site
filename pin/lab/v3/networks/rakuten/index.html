Before you start

A feed pays off when you want many products from one shop, with picture and price. For two or three, a normal Rakuten link in your text is enough. How a shop's product feed gets into WordPress explains the idea.

  • The shop has approved youMyFeeds lists the advertisers you have an active partnership with. Advertiser is Rakuten's word for a shop.
  • MyFeeds Pro is in your WordPressThe direct connection is part of Pro and higher. Paid plans are a plugin of their own, covered in the install guide.
  • You can open Rakuten's developer portalA site of its own, next to your publisher dashboard. Two of your three access details come from there.

Step 1 of 2

Get your access details from Rakuten

GoalThree values, copied and ready to paste.

  1. Open Rakuten's developer portal

    Sign in to Rakuten's developer portal. The MyFeeds dialog in step 2 repeats these steps and links to the portal.

    Menus move. Rakuten's own walkthrough for this step: Access Tokens.

  2. Add an application

    On the Applications page, click Add Application. An application is the portal's word for a connection like this one, and it comes with a Client ID and a Client Secret. Copy both, and treat the secret like a password.

  3. Note your SID

    The SID is your publisher account ID from the Rakuten dashboard: Rakuten's walkthrough says account ID. It is not the ID of the application you just added.

    What you take to WordPress

    Client ID
    from the developer portal
    Client Secret
    from the developer portal, private
    SID
    your publisher account ID

    MyFeeds keeps all three on your own site.

Step 2 of 2

Connect Rakuten to your WordPress

From Pro

Connect Rakuten directly

  1. In WordPress, open MyFeeds and click + Add API source. Paste the three values and click Save and test.
  2. Pick the advertiser. Take everything it lists, or only some categories or keywords.
  3. Name the feed after the shop and click Create feed. One feed holds one shop. For the next shop, the button reads + Rakuten feed.
  • MyFeeds fetches the products again every night. You never handle a file.
Without Pro

Rakuten's file route

Rakuten's Product Catalog puts a file for each shop on an SFTP server. Rakuten says it is meant for publishers with technical resources and does not suit all. Its support sets up the account, then you apply to each shop under Links, Product Feeds.

  • MyFeeds cannot log in to SFTP, and we have not confirmed that it reads a Rakuten file you fetch and upload. Try one shop first.

What MyFeeds gets from Rakuten

Rakuten's product search answers with a short list of fields.

Fields from Rakuten's product search and what a card shows from them
Field from RakutenWhat your card shows
productnameThe product's name
price, salepriceThe price, and the old price crossed out when the shop has reduced it
imageurlThe picture
linkurlWhere a tap leads: the product in the shop, through your affiliate link

No stock field, no brand field. MyFeeds reads the brand from the product name where it can. It cannot tell from this source that a product is sold out.

5,000 products for each query. That is Rakuten's limit (as of October 2026). MyFeeds fetches a larger shop in slices and shows how much it reached. For a very large shop that can be part of the range: choose the categories or keywords you write about.

Questions about Rakuten products

For anything else, write to us.

Why does the advertiser show zero products?

Rakuten's product search returned nothing for it.

MyFeeds can only fetch what that search returns: Rakuten describes it as the product data of the advertisers you partner with. If the advertiser sells products and still shows zero, ask it, or Rakuten's support, whether its product data is open to you.

Does the free plugin work with Rakuten?

Not for the direct connection: that is part of Pro.

The free plugin takes an uploaded file, but Rakuten's text files have no row of column names, which MyFeeds needs to tell the columns apart. We expect them not to import, and the XML version is untested.

More questions

Rakuten rejected my details

Check the Client ID, the Client Secret and the SID. The SID is your publisher account ID, not the ID of the application.

Can I get fresh prices before tonight?

Yes. On the feed's row, click Refresh now. MyFeeds fetches the shop's products from Rakuten again.

Can I see which post and product a click came from?

ProYes: clicks on your cards are counted by post and product. MyFeeds also adds a click ID in u1, a field Rakuten reports back.

Rakuten's help says you can add u1 once your account is enabled for its Signature option. It then appears in the Signature Orders Report as Member ID.

Sources, read in October 2026
