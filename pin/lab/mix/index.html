Questions before you start

Do I need an affiliate network?

Yes, or a program that gives you a product feed. MyFeeds reads the feeds from AWIN, Rakuten Advertising, CJ, Impact, Partnerize, Webgains, Tradedoubler, Admitad, FlexOffers, Sovrn and Pepperjam. If your program publishes a feed file you can download, MyFeeds will almost certainly import it. Amazon has no feed file, so on Pro its products come live from Amazon's own Creators API. Amazon opens that access to associates with ten qualified sales in thirty days.

MyFeeds keeps growing, so that every affiliate can bring in the product feeds of the shops they work with. Is your platform missing? We can connect more platforms on request: write to business@myfeeds.site.

Will it slow down my blog?

No, products are stored in your own WordPress database and the cards are built from there, so your pages don't wait on a third-party server to show them.

Do I need technical skills?

No code, no command line. If you can install a WordPress plugin and paste a link, you're set. The first time you open one of the main MyFeeds screens, a short guided demo walks you through it step by step, and you can replay it any time.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes, any time, right from your WordPress dashboard. Your feeds and posts stay as they are.

What happens after the 7 days?

If you keep Pro, it continues at $19 a month. Cancel from your dashboard before the trial ends and you are not charged. The checkout asks for a card or PayPal to start the trial.

Can I start with the free version?

Yes. The free version covers one feed and the product grid. Get it on WordPress.org.
