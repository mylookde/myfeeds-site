Questions before you start

Do I need an affiliate network?

Yes, or a program that gives you a product feed. MyFeeds reads the feeds from AWIN, Rakuten Advertising, CJ, Impact, Partnerize, Webgains, Tradedoubler, Admitad, FlexOffers, Sovrn and Pepperjam. If your program publishes a feed file you can download, MyFeeds will almost certainly import it.

Will it slow down my blog?

Products are stored in your own WordPress database and the cards are built from there, so your pages don't wait on a third-party server to show them.

What happens after the 7 days?

If you keep Pro, it continues at $19 a month. Cancel from your dashboard before the trial ends and you are not charged. The checkout asks for a card or PayPal to start the trial.

Can I start with the free version?

Yes. The free version covers one feed and the product grid. Get it on WordPress.org.
