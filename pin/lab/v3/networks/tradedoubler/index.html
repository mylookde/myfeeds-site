Before you start

A shop's product feed pays off when you show many of its products, with picture and price. For two or three, a normal Tradedoubler link in your text is enough. The WordPress part is untested with a live Tradedoubler account: start with one shop.

  • The shop has accepted youTradedoubler opens a shop's products once you are connected to its programme. Otherwise the address answers with error PF_392.
  • The shop shares its productsStep 1 lists the feeds your account can read. A shop missing there has nothing to import.
  • MyFeeds is in your WordPressOur plugin for the WordPress block editor. The install guide takes you through setting it up.

Step 1 of 2

Build the feed address in Tradedoubler

GoalOne address that returns the shop's products, your tracking links included, whatever you paste it into.

  1. Copy your products token

    A token is a long key that lets an address read data in your name. Tradedoubler's guide currently puts your tokens under Account, then Manage tokens.

    Copy the token in the row PRODUCTS. The other rows belong to other services.

    Menus move. Another Tradedoubler page says Settings, then Tokens. If you find neither, see Tradedoubler's Products API guide for publishers.

  2. Look up the shop's feed number

    Open the address below in your browser, with your token at the end. Plain data opens, not a web page.

    The list of feeds your account can read

    https://api.tradedoubler.com/1.0/productFeeds?token=YOUR-TOKEN

    name
    Kicks Corner
    feedId
    12345
    numberOfProducts
    2840

    Find the shop and note its two numbers. An invented example.

  3. Put the address together

    The address you paste into WordPress

    https://api.tradedoubler.com/1.0/products;fid=FEED-ID?token=YOUR-TOKEN

    • products is a search, capped at 1,000 products. If numberOfProducts is higher, write productsUnlimited instead: that returns the whole feed.
    • FEED-ID is the feedId you noted. Mind the semicolon before fid and the question mark after the number.
    • YOUR-TOKEN is personal. Treat the address like a password: keep it out of screenshots and forum posts.

You have the address. On to step 2

Step 2 of 2

Add the address in WordPress

GoalThe shop's products in WordPress, and one card checked.

Paste the address

  1. In WordPress, open MyFeeds and click Add your first feed. On a paid plan, later feeds go through + Add Feed.
  2. Name the feed after the shop: readers see that name on your cards. Keep At a URL, paste the address and click Create Feed.
  3. Let it import in the background while you keep working. When it is done, the feed's row shows the number of products. If it is far below numberOfProducts, see the questions below.
Untested with a live Tradedoubler account

Check one card before you publish

Put one product into a draft and look at the card.

  • It shows a price and a picture.
  • A tap opens that product in the shop.

In Tradedoubler's own example, a product's ID, price and tracking link sit one level down, under offers. We could not confirm that MyFeeds finds them there, so the import may show 0 products.

If it does, or a card lacks its price or link, don't publish. Tell us the shop and what you see: support@myfeeds.site.

One card checks out. Publish your first cards

How your cards stay current

Every night

Price, crossed-out price, stock, picture and link of the products in your published posts.

Once a week

The whole feed. New products become searchable. A product the shop has removed disappears from your posts.

The whole-feed address has a download limit. Three downloads of the same file version in 24 hours (as of October 2026). Adding the feed, each Reimport and the nightly read all count, so click Reimport once a day at most.

Questions about Tradedoubler feeds

For anything else, write to us.

Why do I never get more than 1,000 products?

Because the address says products. Tradedoubler stops that search at 1,000, and a second page returns nothing (as of October 2026).

Write productsUnlimited instead, as in step 1. Or narrow the search: Tradedoubler's guide lists keys for a keyword, a brand and a price.

The whole-feed address answers with error 429

Wait 24 hours, then click Reimport once.

The download limit is used up. Tradedoubler lifts it as soon as the shop's feed changes, and it does not apply in your first five days with a feed.

More questions

Tradedoubler says my token is invalid

You have most likely copied the token of another service. Take the one in the row PRODUCTS.

With “Token not found”, the end of the address is missing. It has to finish with ?token= and your token.

The import brought far fewer products than the feed has

Tradedoubler's guide does not say how many products a search returns by default. Its keys for the amount are pageSize and limit. In MyFeeds, click Edit on the feed's row, add ;pageSize=1000;limit=1000 after the feed number and click Save Changes, then Reimport. We could not test this.

Can I see which post and product a click came from?

ProFrom the Pro plan, yes: clicks on your cards are counted by post and product. Sales and commissions you read in your Tradedoubler reports, as before.
