Install the plugin

  1. In WordPress, open Plugins and click Add New Plugin.
  2. Search for MyFeeds. The one with the violet icon is ours. Click Install Now.
  3. Click Activate. MyFeeds now sits in your WordPress sidebar.

Choose where your products come from

MyFeeds shows products from shops you're an affiliate for. They arrive in one of three ways.

Most networks

A shop's product feed

Your affiliate network gives you a link to each shop's product list, with prices and pictures. Copy it for step 3.

A link beats a file: a file you upload stays as it was that day, a link is fetched again every night.

No feed link

Rakuten Advertising

Rakuten Advertising hands out no feed link: MyFeeds connects to its API instead, with the advertisers you're partnered with.

You need the Client ID, Client Secret and SID from Rakuten's developer portal. How to get them.

No feed file

Amazon

Amazon has no feed file. On every plan, the free plugin included, you add an Amazon product by link or ASIN with your own partner tag: it shows the name you give it and links to Amazon, without picture or price.

Once Amazon opens its own Creators API to you, the product cards in your posts show Amazon's picture and price, live: in the free plugin as your one product source, in Pro beside your feeds. Amazon opens that access to associates with ten qualified sales in thirty days.

Free plugin: one product source, a feed, Rakuten or Amazon's API. Pro and Shop: every source at once.

No feed yet? On the empty MyFeeds screen, click Load 7 sample products and go straight to step 4.

Network not on the list? Most other CSV, TSV, XML and JSON feeds work too, and we connect further platforms on request: support@myfeeds.site.

3Step 3 of 4

Add it in WordPress

No settings to work out: MyFeeds recognises the format and finds the title, price, picture and link on its own.

A feed link

  1. Click MyFeeds in the sidebar, then + Add a feed in the free plugin or + Add your first feed in Pro. Later feeds go through + Add Feed.
  2. Give it a name you'll recognise, the shop's name for example.
  3. Under Where is the feed? keep At a URL, paste the link into Feed URL and click Create Feed.
  4. The import runs in the background. You can keep working or close the page.

Rakuten or Amazon's API

  1. Rakuten: click + Add API source, enter the Client ID, Client Secret and SID, and choose the advertisers whose products you want.
  2. Amazon: add products by link or ASIN right in the product picker, with your partner tag. Once Amazon opens its API to you, enter the credential id and secret in MyFeeds and click Save and test.

A column comes in empty? Some shops name a column in a way MyFeeds has never seen. The mapping editor lets you point it at the right field.

4Step 4 of 4

Publish your first product cards

In any post in the block editor.

  1. On a new line type /myfeeds and choose MyFeeds – Product Picker.

  2. Type a product type or a brand and click Search Products.

  3. Click the products you want, then Use 3 Products. The number follows your picks.

  4. Publish. Readers click through with your affiliate link, and prices refresh every night.

The same block also shows a carousel, a recommendation card or a comparison table, and the Shop the look block puts products on your own photo.

Your first product cards are live. That's the whole setup.

If something goes wrong

The snags people run into most, and the fix for each.

MyFeeds doesn't show up in the plugin searchFree

Your site may not be able to reach wordpress.org. Download the ZIP from the plugin page and upload it under Plugins, Add New Plugin, Upload Plugin.

The email with my download link never arrivedPaid

Check your spam folder. Then sign in at users.freemius.com: every active license has a Download button and its license key there.

MyFeeds Pro won't activate, or shows an errorPaid

The free plugin was still active. The two can't run at the same time. Deactivate the free MyFeeds first, then activate MyFeeds Pro. Your feeds, products and posts stay. Stuck in the admin? Write to support@myfeeds.site.

I can't find my shop's product feed link

Open the guide for your network. You need to be accepted into the shop's affiliate program first; then the network lists the shop's feed for you. Rakuten has none: connect it with + Add API source instead.

The import seems to take forever

It runs in the background, and how long it takes depends on the size of the shop's feed. You can keep working or close the page meanwhile.

Some of the shop's products are missing

MyFeeds needs a product ID in every row of the feed; rows without one are skipped. If a title, price or picture comes in empty, the column has a name MyFeeds doesn't know yet: point it at the right field in the mapping editor.

Prices on my cards don't change

The feed was probably added as an uploaded file, and a file stays as it was that day. Add it again with At a URL and the link from your network: MyFeeds fetches a link every night. You can also sync any feed by hand.

I can't find the Product Picker block

MyFeeds needs the WordPress block editor; with the Classic Editor the block isn't there. In the block editor, type /myfeeds on a new line.

My license won't activate on a second sitePaid

One license covers one site. Choose All my sites in the plans, or write to support@myfeeds.site.

Do I need technical skills?

No code and no command line. If you can install a WordPress plugin and paste a link, you're set. In Pro, a short guided demo walks you through each MyFeeds screen the first time you open it.
