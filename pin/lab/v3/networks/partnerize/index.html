Before you start

A feed pays off when you show many products from one shop, with picture and price. For two or three, a normal Partnerize link in your text does the job. How a shop's product feed gets into WordPress explains the idea.

  • The shop has accepted youPartnerize calls a shop a brand and its programme a campaign.
  • The shop shares a product feedThat is each shop's choice, so a campaign without a feed is normal.
  • MyFeeds is in your WordPressOur plugin for the WordPress block editor. If it is not installed yet, the install guide takes you through it.

Step 1 of 2

Copy the feed link in Partnerize

GoalOne link to the shop's product feed, ready to paste.

  1. Open your feed list

    Partnerize documents one address that lists the product feeds shops have shared with you. Open it in your browser.

    The address of your feed list

    https://api.partnerize.com/user/publisher/YOUR-PUBLISHER-ID/feed.json

    • YOUR-PUBLISHER-ID is your publisher ID, not the user ID on your account page. A 2023 walkthrough (source 4) places it under Partner settings.
    • Your browser asks for a username and a password: your User application key and your User API key, both from Account settings. Treat them like passwords.
    • The answer is raw text. In it, each feed has a name and a location. The location is the link.

    From Partnerize's API documentation, source 1.

    There may be a screen for this too. We could not confirm where the Partnerize console lists product feeds, or what it calls them. The address above is the documented way.

  2. Pick the feed for your readers' country

    A shop often has one feed for each country it sells in, named after the country. Take the one most of your readers shop in. Your cards show that feed's prices: MyFeeds converts nothing.

  3. Copy the link

    Copy the whole link. In the feed list, location_compressed is the same feed, compressed. Either works.

You have the link copied. On to step 2

Step 2 of 2

Add the link in WordPress

GoalThe shop's products in WordPress, ready to search in the editor.
Recommended

Paste the link

  1. In WordPress, open MyFeeds and click Add your first feed. On a paid plan, later feeds go through + Add Feed.
  2. Name the feed after the shop: readers see that name on your cards. Keep At a URL, paste the link and click Create Feed.
  3. Let it import. It runs in the background while you keep working.
  • A link is read again every night, so the cards in your published posts follow the feed's prices.
If MyFeeds shows an error for the link

Upload the file

  1. Open the feed link in your browser and save the file.
  2. In the same MyFeeds dialog, choose Upload a file.
  • A file stays as it was on upload day, until you upload a newer one.

What a Partnerize feed gives you

The shop builds the feed and decides what is in it. Partnerize hosts it.

What a Partnerize product feed contains
In the feedOn Partnerize
Product linksAlready your tracking links. Partnerize puts each partner's tracking into the feed.
Format and columnsThe shop's choice. MyFeeds reads CSV, XML and JSON, compressed or not, and matches the columns itself. If a field stays empty on your cards, set it in the Mapping Editor.
How freshThe shop sets how often Partnerize fetches its products. Your cards can only be as current as that.

Use the feed from your own account. The links in it are tracked for you. In a feed someone else passes on, the commission is theirs.

Once a week, the whole feed. New products become searchable then, and products the shop has removed leave your posts.

Questions about Partnerize feeds

For anything else, write to us.

My campaign shows no product feed. What now?

Then the shop has not shared one. Partnerize leaves that to each shop.

Ask the shop, or your contact at Partnerize, whether one is planned. Until then, link to its products by hand.

Is Partnerize the same as Pepperjam or Ascend?

No. Ascend, the former Pepperjam network, belongs to Partnerize but hands out its feeds differently.

Look at one of your affiliate links. If it runs through prf.hn, you are on Partnerize. If it runs through pntra.com, you are on Ascend: see the Pepperjam guide.

More questions

Does the feed link expire?

We found no expiry in Partnerize's documentation, and no promise either. The feed links we have seen stay the same and open without a login.

If MyFeeds ever shows an error for it, copy the link again in Partnerize. In MyFeeds, click Edit on the feed's row, swap the link, then click Reimport.

Can I see which post and product a click came from?

ProFrom the Pro plan, yes: clicks on your cards are counted by post and product. MyFeeds also adds a click ID to each Partnerize link, as pubref, the publisher reference Partnerize reports back.
