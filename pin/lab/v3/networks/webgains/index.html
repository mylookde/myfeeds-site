Before you start

A shop's product feed pays off when you show many of its products, with picture and price. For two or three, a link from Webgains' Deep Link Creator is enough.

  • The programme says JoinedWebgains lists a feed only for programmes you have joined. An application alone is not enough.
  • You are in the shop's country networkFor a shop from another country, apply to that country's Webgains network first.
  • MyFeeds is in your WordPressOur plugin for the WordPress block editor. The install guide takes you through setting it up.

Step 1 of 2

Get the feed link in Webgains

GoalOne link to the shop's product feed, checked and ready to paste, whatever you paste it into.

  1. Find the shop's feed

    Open the menu that lists the product feeds of your programmes. Webgains currently calls it Product Feeds. Use its filters to find the shop.

    Menus move. If it is gone, see Webgains' own guide to exporting feeds.

  2. Select categories and columns

    Select the product categories you write about, or all of them. Tick the optional columns the shop has added: a card can only show what the feed carries.

  3. Choose CSV

    Webgains offers CSV, XML and TSV. Take CSV: it opens in any spreadsheet, which you need for the check below.

  4. Copy the link and check it once

    Webgains now offers to copy or download the URL. Copy it.

    Webgains says it turns shop addresses into tracking links when it processes a feed. We could not confirm that, so look once. A plain shop address would earn you nothing: if you find one, ask Webgains first.

The link checks out. On to step 2

Step 2 of 2

Add the link in WordPress

GoalThe shop's products in WordPress, ready to search in the editor.
Try this first

Paste the link

  1. In WordPress, open MyFeeds and click Add your first feed. On a paid plan, later feeds go through + Add Feed.
  2. Name the feed after the shop: readers see that name on your cards. Keep At a URL, paste the link and click Create Feed.
  3. Let it import in the background while you keep working. When it is done, the feed's row shows the number of products.
  • MyFeeds reads a link again every night.
If the link asks for a login or fails

Upload the file

  1. In Webgains, download the file instead.
  2. In the same MyFeeds dialog, choose Upload a file.
  • A file stays as it was on upload day. Prices and stock freeze until you upload a newer one.

The shop's products are in. Publish your first cards

What a Webgains feed contains

Webgains asks every shop for the same columns.

Columns of a Webgains feed and what a product card shows from them
Column in the feedWhat your card shows
titleThe product's name
brandThe brand, above the name
priceThe price
sale_priceThe price during a sale, with price crossed out beside it
image_linkThe picture
linkWhere a tap leads: the product in the shop, through your tracking link

Check your first card. We could not test this with a live Webgains account. Look for the price, the picture, and a tap that opens the product.

How it stays current. Every night MyFeeds refreshes the products in your published posts: price, stock, picture, link. Once a week it reads the whole feed: new products become searchable, removed ones leave your posts. Shops update their Webgains feed daily at most.

Questions about Webgains feeds

For anything else, write to us.

Why is there no feed for the shop I applied to?

Look up the programme's status in your Programs list.

Webgains programme statuses and what to do with each
StatusWhat to do
PendingWait. The shop has not decided yet.
Not joinedApply. Some shops approve at once.
RejectedWait 90 days, then use Rejoin (as of October 2026).
SuspendedContact the shop or your Webgains contact.

Will the link I copied keep working?

Webgains' help does not say, and we could not confirm it.

If an import fails one day, copy a fresh link in Webgains. In MyFeeds, click Edit on the feed's row, swap the link and click Save Changes, then Reimport. If that fails too, upload the file.

More questions

A sale I referred was not tracked. Why?

Webgains names the usual reasons: the buyer clicked another site's link last, declined or deleted cookies, or bought after the cookie window, 30 days by default. Sometimes the shop's own tracking is broken.

If you are sure the sale came through your link, raise a Transaction Enquiry with the programme (as of October 2026).

Can I see which post and product a click came from?

ProFrom the Pro plan, yes: clicks on your cards are counted by post and product. MyFeeds adds no click ID to a Webgains link. Sales and commissions you read in your Webgains reports, as before.
