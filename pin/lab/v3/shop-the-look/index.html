How it works with Amazon

“Where did you get that?”

Your photo is why readers want the lamp. Getting to it should be as easy as looking at it.

  • A list under the photo. Your reader works out which link is the lamp.
  • A widget from someone else's platform. Collective Voice, once ShopStyle Collective, has closed.
  • A price typed by hand. It was right on the day you published.

Now the photo answers.

A tap opens a small card: the name, the shop's current price, your note and the way to the shop.

  • The list under the photo is easy to tap on a phone.
  • The look is part of your post, not a widget loaded from a platform.
  • When a shop's product sells out or is gone, its dot leaves the photo on its own.

Four steps in the WordPress block editor.

For shop products you need the shop's product feed: its product list, with your affiliate links in it. Most affiliate networks offer one. See which networks. Amazon dots need no feed.

  1. Choose a photo

    Pick one from your media library: your own, or one you have the right to use.

  2. Click on the photo

    Each click sets a numbered dot, up to 30. Drag to move it.

  3. floor lamp

    Fill one field per dot

    Type a product's name to search the shops' feeds, or paste the link to its page at the shop. The Pro plan takes Amazon links too.

  4. Publish

    Readers reach each shop through your own affiliate link.

Your photo is the picture. Amazon is the link.

A dot can also lead to an Amazon product. Paste its full address (not an amzn.to short link) or its ASIN, Amazon's product number, and type a name.

  • The dot shows the name you typed, your note and a link to the Amazon site your tag belongs to.
  • Your own Associates tag is enough, without access to Amazon's product data (the API).
  • The dot never shows Amazon's photo or price. Amazon's rules don't let a site store its photos, and prices must come from Amazon's API.
  • MyFeeds cannot see when an Amazon product is gone. Check those dots yourself now and then.

Questions before you start

Can I try it first?

Tap the examples on this page. To build your own, start the 7-day Starter trial; the checkout asks for a card or PayPal. The free plugin has product cards (shop products shown in your posts), not the look.

Which plan do I need?

Starter, $9 a month, for looks with shop products. Pro, $19 a month, once a dot should lead to Amazon. Only Amazon? Then Pro, and you need no feed.

Is this allowed by Amazon and Pinterest?

Links on your own site are what Amazon's programme is made for. The disclosures stay your job: MyFeeds adds none. A Pin that leads to your post carries no affiliate link itself; our guide to affiliate links on Pinterest has the rest. This is not legal advice.
