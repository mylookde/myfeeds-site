Before you start

A downloaded file keeps the prices of the day you saved it, so it suits shops whose prices rarely move. For two or three products, an Ascend link in your text is enough. How a shop's product feed gets into WordPress explains the idea.

  • The shop's programme has approved youAscend lists feeds only for programmes you have joined. It calls shops advertisers.
  • The shop has a product feedAscend recommends one to shops but does not require it.
  • MyFeeds is in your WordPressOur plugin for the WordPress block editor. If it is not installed yet, the install guide takes you through it.

Step 1 of 2

Download the feed in Ascend

GoalOne shop's product feed as a file, with one of its links checked.

  1. Open the list of product feeds

    It shows the programmes you have joined that have a product feed. Ascend currently calls it Products and keeps it in the Creatives tab.

    Menus move. If it is gone, see Ascend's own answer: Where do I locate product feeds?

  2. Filter for one shop

    Use the Advertiser filter on the left and take one shop at a time. In MyFeeds a feed has one name, and readers see it as the shop on every card.

  3. Download the feed

    With a filter set, Download Feed is in the right-hand corner. Save the file. A link at the top of the page downloads all feeds at once: skip it.

  4. Check one link before you upload

    Open the file in a spreadsheet or a text editor and look at one product's link.

    Tracked or plain? Look at how the link starts

    Tracked
    https://pntra.com/t/…
    Plain
    https://theshop.com/…
    • pntra.com is a tracking address from Ascend's own help. A link that starts like your other Ascend links is tracked.
    • theshop.com at the start means a plain link. A reader can buy through it and you get no commission.

    Invented, shortened examples.

    We couldn't confirm which one you get. Ascend's help doesn't say whether the file carries your tracking links. If yours are plain, don't upload the file: ask Ascend support for the feed with tracking.

The file is saved and its links are tracked. On to step 2

Step 2 of 2

Upload the file in WordPress

GoalThe shop's products in WordPress, ready to search in the editor.
The first time

Upload the file

  1. In WordPress, open MyFeeds and click Add your first feed. On a paid plan, later feeds go through + Add Feed.
  2. Name the feed after the shop: readers see that name on your cards. Choose Upload a file, pick the file and click Create Feed.
  3. Let it import. It runs in the background while you keep working.
When prices have moved

Replace the file

  1. Download the feed again in Ascend.
  2. In MyFeeds, click Edit on the feed's row, choose the new file and save.
  3. Click Reimport.
  • In between, your cards keep the prices and stock of upload day. MyFeeds labels such a feed “does not refresh”.

The shop's products are in. Publish your first cards

What you get, and what stays open

Ascend's help says where the file is. It says little about what is in it.

From Ascend's help

What is certain

  • The list holds the programmes you have joined that have a product feed.
  • With a filter set, Download Feed downloads that shop's feed.
  • Ascend processes shops' feeds once a day.
We couldn't confirm

What stays open

  • The file's format. MyFeeds reads CSV, tab-separated text, XML and JSON, zipped or not.
  • Whether its links are your tracking links. See step 1.
  • Whether Ascend's API, its interface for developers, can serve as a feed link. Its JSON and XML answers stop at 2,500 products per request.

Questions about Pepperjam and Ascend feeds

For anything else, write to us.

Is Pepperjam the same as Ascend? And as Partnerize?

Pepperjam and Ascend are one network. Its full name today is Ascend by Partnerize.

Partnerize also runs a platform under its own name, and that one gives you feed links. If your affiliate links run through prf.hn, use the Partnerize guide. Links through pntra.com belong here.

Why is a shop I joined missing from the list?

Either its programme has not approved you yet, or the shop has no product feed.

Once a shop has approved you, its links appear under Creatives. Links but no feed means the shop has none.

More questions

Do I have to upload a new file every time?

Yes, each time you want newer prices. An uploaded file never refreshes itself.

If you can keep the file at an https address of your own, paste that address under At a URL instead. MyFeeds then reads it every night, and you replace the file in that one place.

Can I see which post and product a click came from?

ProFrom the Pro plan, yes: clicks on your cards are counted by post and product. When a card's link is an Ascend tracking link, MyFeeds also adds a click ID in sid, the field Ascend shows in its Transaction Details report.
